For site operators

Verify a token server-side. Do not put it in a browser bundle or a log.

curl -fsS -X POST {{ origin }}/api/v1/earn/premium/verify \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"token":"kprem_…"}'

Response: {"active":true,"site":"premium","plan":"premium","expires_at":"…"}. Cache the positive answer for at most five minutes, keyed by a hash of the token. A false answer is final for that request. To add a site, create its reward in the operator console and make it call this endpoint.
