Terms

Plain terms

Premium rewards instead of payment, no cash value, no warranty, and you can stop at any moment.

What you are doing

You are lending spare compute to the Kituwa cluster. The cluster schedules container workloads onto your machine. You keep ownership of the machine, its data and its network.

  • No cash compensation. Contributing is a donation. There is no token, no equity, no revenue share and no cash payout. Measured work can earn points that are redeemable only for Kituwa Premium under the programme rules.
  • You can leave. Run the uninstall command or simply turn the machine off. Pods drain elsewhere; no notice required.
  • No warranty. The software is provided as-is under the MIT licence. It runs workloads, which uses CPU, memory, disk and bandwidth.
  • Don't do harm. Do not modify the agent to attack the cluster, other nodes, or anyone else, and do not use the cluster's access to reach networks you do not own.

The Earn programme

An Earn account is optional. Points are a loyalty credit with no cash value. They are not money, a security, a token on a chain or an investment, and they cannot be sold or transferred. The only reward is digital Kituwa Premium: no physical goods, no shipping and no address collection.

Every 384 measured CPU-hours of work is one point, worth $1 of Kituwa credit. At the published rate a fully busy 16-thread machine earns at most about $1/day, and a 2-thread laptop about $0.125/day. Only measured work counts; there are no flat bonuses or backdated balances. A node must be linked by its operator with a one-time claim code. The operator may adjust a balance with a written reason, cancel a redemption for a refund, or revoke premium for abuse. Every adjustment and cancellation is recorded in the audit log. Programme rules and prices can change; accrued points are honoured for redemption while the programme runs.

What Kituwa promises

Workloads scheduled on a volunteer node are ordinary container workloads: no access to your home directory, no host mounts, no shell account. Access to the node is limited to the control plane, and the invitation can be revoked at any time.

Kituwa may pause or remove a node from the cluster for operational or security reasons. You will not be asked to pay for anything, ever, to keep contributing.
