Terms

Plain terms

No payment, no promise of payment, no warranty, and you can stop at any moment.

What you are doing

You are lending spare compute to the Kituwa cluster. The cluster schedules container workloads onto your machine. You keep ownership of the machine, its data and its network.

  • No compensation. Contributing is a donation. There is no token, no equity, no revenue share and no contract. If a reward programme exists later, it will be announced on this page and will not apply retroactively.
  • You can leave. Run the uninstall command or simply turn the machine off. Pods drain elsewhere; no notice required.
  • No warranty. The software is provided as-is under the MIT licence. It runs workloads, which uses CPU, memory, disk and bandwidth.
  • Don't do harm. Do not modify the agent to attack the cluster, other nodes, or anyone else, and do not use the cluster's access to reach networks you do not own.

What Kituwa promises

Workloads scheduled on a volunteer node are ordinary container workloads: no access to your home directory, no host mounts, no shell account. Access to the node is limited to the control plane, and the invitation can be revoked at any time.

Kituwa may pause or remove a node from the cluster for operational or security reasons. You will not be asked to pay for anything, ever, to keep contributing.
