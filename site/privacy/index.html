Privacy

What is collected

Short version: page views on the public pages, account data only if you create one for Earn, nothing sold, and no third-party tracker. The installer collects nothing at all.

This website

Two analytics tools run here, both hosted by Kituwa: Matomo at analytics.shopdigital.art and Plausible at plausible.kituwa.com. They record page views, the referring site, approximate country and device type. They are configured without cookies, so there is no identifier that follows you across sites, and no data is shared with a third party.

Server logs on the host record the usual request line — IP address, path, user agent, timestamp — and are used for security and keeping the service up.

Member accounts (Earn)

Creating an account is optional and only needed for the Earn programme. We store the email address you enter, a display name, an Argon2id hash of your password (never the password), the public half of any passkey you add, and the session cookie that keeps you signed in. The cookie is host-only, Secure and HttpOnly.

When you link a node we store its cluster node name, the linking time and the published points total at that moment. The ledger records the points credited, redeemed or adjusted, with a reference and a reason. Premium redemptions store a hash and an encrypted copy of the subscription token so the account can show it to you. There are no shipping addresses and no physical orders.

Security records keep the IP address and user agent of a session and the operator actions taken on an account. They are used to rate-limit abuse, investigate fraud and answer a deletion request. Earn account pages carry no third-party analytics script.

The installer

The launcher makes one request to this site for a one-use join key. It sends only a sanitised hostname so the node has a name; the key is used once and is not stored in your browser. The installer creates no account and sends no telemetry. To join, your machine connects to two things and nothing else:

  • This site, to download the script and ask for the one-use join key.
  • The cluster over the tailnet, plus Tailscale's own coordination service to establish that private link.

Your hostname is used as the node name in the cluster. Nothing in your home directory is read or uploaded.

Leaving and removal

Running the uninstall command removes the agent, its config and, with --remove-tailscale, the tailnet login. To delete an Earn account and its ledger, use the contact page from the account's email address and name the account. An operator will remove the account, its sessions, passkeys and unspent balance; redemption records can be kept in a minimal audit form where needed to prevent fraud.

Questions, or a request to delete something: services.kituwa.com/quote.

Who runs this

This site and the cluster are operated by Kituwa. The quickest contact is the contact form; it reaches the same inbox as the rest of the estate. Legal entity and postal address are available on request. No account is required to ask a question or to leave.
